*Crisp revision notes*

## Content (20 Qs)

**Number System**
- Knowing our numbers, place value, playing with numbers (factors, multiples, divisibility rules).
- **Whole numbers** (0,1,2,3…) vs. **Integers** (…,-2,-1,0,1,2,…) — whole numbers don't include negatives.
- Properties: closure, commutativity, associativity, distributivity.
  - e.g. `2 × (3+4) = 2×3 + 2×4` → distributive property.
  - e.g. `5+3 = 3+5` → commutative property.

**Fractions**
- Proper (num < denom), improper (num ≥ denom), equivalence, comparison, basic operations.

**Algebra**
- **Introduction to Algebra**: using a letter/variable for an unknown in a simple expression or equation.
- Expression (`x+5`) vs. Equation (`x+5=12`) — an equation has an "=" sign.
- **Ratio & Proportion**: unitary method (find value of 1 unit first); direct proportion (both increase together) vs. inverse proportion (one increases, other decreases — e.g. more workers → less time for a fixed task).

**Geometry**
- Basic 2-D ideas: points, lines, angles.
- Understanding elementary shapes (2-D and 3-D) — e.g. circle (2-D) vs. sphere (3-D); a solid's **net** = its unfolded 2-D layout.
- **Symmetry** (reflection): one half mirrors the other across a line (e.g. butterfly wings).
- **Construction**: using ruler, protractor, compass to draw accurate figures.
- **Mensuration**: perimeter, area, volume.
- **Data Handling**: bar graphs, pictographs, mean/median/mode basics.

## Pedagogical Issues (10 Qs)

- **Nature of Mathematics/Logical thinking**: maths = a tool for logical thinking, not just calculation.
- **Place of Mathematics in Curriculum**: reflecting on *why* maths matters in overall education, not just exam weightage.
- **Language of Mathematics**: symbols are a language; mismatches between everyday language ("take away") and formal maths language ("subtract") cause errors.
- **Community Mathematics**: connecting classroom maths to everyday/local practices (e.g. how a shopkeeper calculates change).
- **Evaluation**: should test understanding & application, not just calculation speed.
- **Remedial Teaching**: diagnose the *specific* error pattern (e.g. place-value confusion) and use a targeted alternative approach — not generic re-teaching.
- **Problem of Teaching**: the genuine challenge of making abstract maths concepts accessible to children.

---

### How this section is usually tested
Content questions are mostly direct (identify the property/concept/correct calculation). Pedagogy questions are scenario-based — "a teacher does X, which pedagogical issue does this reflect?" Know the *names* of these issues (Community Mathematics, Language of Mathematics, Remedial Teaching) well enough to match them to a described classroom situation.
