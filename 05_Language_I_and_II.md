# CTET Paper II — Language I & Language II (30 + 30 Questions each)
*Crisp revision notes — the two sections share an identical structure and pedagogy content; only the comprehension passages/language differ.*

---

## Language Comprehension (15 Qs each section)

- **Language I**: two unseen passages — one prose/drama + one poem — with questions on comprehension, inference, grammar, verbal ability. Prose may be literary, scientific, narrative, or **discursive** (argumentative/opinion-based).
- **Language II**: two unseen prose passages (discursive/literary/narrative/scientific) with questions on comprehension, grammar, verbal ability.
- Practice **inference** (reading between the lines, not just literal recall) and **vocabulary-in-context** — these are the most commonly tested skill types, more than pure grammar rules.
- Grammar is usually tested **embedded in the passage** (e.g. "which verb form is used correctly in the passage"), not as an isolated worksheet-style drill.

---

## Pedagogy of Language Development (15 Qs each section)

- **Learning vs. Acquisition**: acquisition = natural, implicit (like first-language development); learning = conscious, structured. CTET expects you to know this distinction.
- **Listening & speaking precede reading & writing** developmentally — oral competence is the foundation; language is a functional "tool" children use to think and communicate from an early age.
- **IT as a tool**: thoughtful use of digital/multimedia resources in language teaching (not avoidance, not over-reliance).
- **Critical perspective on grammar**: teach grammar contextually/functionally for real communication — not through isolated rule drills.
- **Challenges of teaching language in a diverse classroom**: language difficulties, errors, and disorders need sensitive, differentiated responses — not harsh correction or exclusion.
- **Language Skills**: the four skills — **L**istening, **S**peaking, **R**eading, **W**riting (LSRW) — all four must be evaluated, not writing alone.
- **Evaluating language comprehension & proficiency**: across all four LSRW skills.
- **Teaching-learning materials**: a mix of textbook + multimedia + the classroom's own **multilingual resources**.
- **Multilingual classroom**: treated as a *resource* to bridge learning (using children's home languages), not a barrier or problem.
- **Remedial Teaching**: differentiated, evidence-informed support for specific language difficulties.

---

### How this section is usually tested
Comprehension questions are largely unseen-passage based, so there's limited "content" to memorise — focus practice time on speed-reading + inference technique instead. Pedagogy questions repeat the same handful of ideas every cycle: acquisition-vs-learning, LSRW evaluation, multilingual classrooms as a resource, and contextual (not drill-based) grammar teaching. Knowing these four well covers most of the pedagogy marks in both Language I and Language II.
