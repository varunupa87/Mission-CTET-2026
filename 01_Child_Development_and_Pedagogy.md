*Crisp revision notes — theory + how it's usually tested*

## A. Child Development (15 Qs)

**Growth vs. Development**
- Growth = quantitative, measurable (height, weight).
- Development = quantitative + qualitative, lifelong, continuous, sequential — not random.
- Follows **cephalocaudal** (head→toe) and **proximodistal** (centre→periphery) patterns.

**Heredity & Environment**
- Heredity sets potential/limits; environment determines how much is realised.
- Modern (and CTET's) view: continuous **interaction**, not either/or.

**Piaget — Cognitive Development** (child as a "lone scientist" constructing knowledge)

| Stage | Age | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| Sensorimotor | 0–2 | Object permanence |
| Preoperational | 2–7 | Egocentrism, symbolic play, no conservation |
| Concrete Operational | 7–11 | Conservation, reversibility, logic on *concrete* objects |
| Formal Operational | 11+ | Abstract, hypothetical reasoning |

**Vygotsky — Sociocultural Theory** (child as a "social being")
- **ZPD**: gap between what a child can do alone vs. with guidance.
- **Scaffolding**: temporary support from a "More Knowledgeable Other" (MKO), withdrawn as competence grows.
- Language = a tool for thought. Social interaction *drives* development (opposite emphasis to Piaget).

**Kohlberg — Moral Development** (3 levels, 6 stages)
1. Pre-conventional — punishment-avoidance → self-interest
2. Conventional — good boy/girl → law & order
3. Post-conventional — social contract → universal ethical principles

**Gardner — Multiple Intelligence**
- Intelligence is plural: linguistic, logical-mathematical, spatial, musical, bodily-kinesthetic, interpersonal, intrapersonal, naturalistic.
- Critiques single-score IQ testing.

**Socialization**: family → peers → teachers → community, in widening circles — each shapes the child's "social world."

**Gender**: treated as a **social construct**, not biology — classrooms must actively counter gender-bias in materials, seating, expectations.

**Individual Differences**: from language, caste, gender, community, religion, socio-economic background — pedagogy must differentiate, not standardise.

**Assessment**
- *Assessment FOR learning* = Formative — ongoing, diagnostic, improves learning.
- *Assessment OF learning* = Summative — final, certifies achievement.
- **CCE** (Continuous & Comprehensive Evaluation): continuous (regular, not just term-end) + comprehensive (scholastic + co-scholastic).
- Good questions test *readiness* and *critical thinking*, not rote recall.

## B. Inclusive Education & CWSN (5 Qs)

- Inclusion = educating **all** children (disadvantaged, deprived, disabled, gifted) in the **same** mainstream classroom, with need-based support — not segregated schools.
- **Impairment** = loss/abnormality of body structure/function; **Disability** = the resulting activity limitation — know this distinction.
- Gifted/talented/creative learners need **enrichment**, not just acceleration.
- Disadvantaged/deprived learners need equitable access and support (RTE Act, 2009) — not lowered expectations.

## C. Learning & Pedagogy (10 Qs)

- Children as **problem-solvers and "scientific investigators"** — learning by doing, not passive listening.
- **Errors ≠ failure** — they reveal a child's thinking process (constructivist view); "alternative conceptions" are natural, addressable steps.
- Learning is a **social activity** — happens in context, not isolation.
- **Cognition & Emotion** are linked — anxiety hampers learning; positive affect aids it.
- **Motivation**: intrinsic (interest-driven) > extrinsic (reward-driven) for deep, lasting learning.
- Factors affecting learning: personal (motivation, readiness, prior knowledge) + environmental (classroom climate, teacher behaviour, resources).

### How this section is usually tested
Almost every past cycle leans on **scenario-based** questions: a classroom situation is described, and you identify *whose theory* or *which concept* it illustrates. Memorising the theorist-to-concept pairing (Piaget=stages, Vygotsky=ZPD/scaffolding, Kohlberg=moral stages, Gardner=multiple intelligence) is the single highest-leverage thing you can revise here.
