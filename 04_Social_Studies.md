## Geography (part of the 40 Content Qs)

- Geography as **both** a social study and a science.
- Earth in the solar system; the **Globe** — latitude/longitude used to locate any place precisely.
- Environment in its totality: **natural + human** together (not just untouched nature).
- **Air** — atmosphere's composition, role in climate/weather.
- **Water**.
- **Human Environment**: settlement, transport, communication patterns shaped by people.
- **Resources**: natural (e.g. rivers, forests) vs. human (e.g. skilled labour) — know this distinction.
- **Agriculture**: farming practices linked to geography/climate.

## Pedagogical Issues (20 Qs)

- **Nature of Social Science/Social Studies**: interdisciplinary (History + Geography + Political Science + Economics); concerned with human society across time and space.
- **Classroom Processes, activities and discourse**: interactive discussion and activities, not silent lecturing.
- **Developing Critical Thinking**: encouraging multiple viewpoints, not one "correct" narrative.
- **Enquiry/Empirical Evidence**: conclusions based on evidence, not assumption.
- **Sources — Primary & Secondary**: primary = original materials from the period (documents, artefacts, coins); secondary = textbook accounts/analysis written later.
- **Projects Work**: real-world connections (e.g. mapping the local neighbourhood).
- **Evaluation**: test analysis and understanding, not just dates/facts.
- **Problems of teaching Social Science/Social Studies**: e.g. making abstract concepts (democracy, justice) relatable.
